Eyewash

LP11X / LP11E

Wall-mounted eyewash station

Wall-mounted eyewash in stainless steel (LP11X) and epoxy-coated galvanized steel (LP11E)

Designed to ANSI/ISEA Z358.1-2014

The wall-mounted eyewash is fixed directly to the wall, with no base, and takes up little space. It comes in two versions: LP11X, in stainless steel, and LP11E, in galvanized steel with yellow thermo-epoxy paint. Both have a mirror-polished stainless steel eyewash bowl and are activated with a push plate; the valves stay open until the user closes them. With adequate supply pressure, it delivers 1.5 L/min for 15 minutes.

Technical specifications

VersionsLP11X (stainless steel) and LP11E (galvanized steel with yellow thermo-epoxy paint)
PipingLP11X: 1" and ½" stainless steel. LP11E: 1" epoxy-coated galvanized steel and, at the eyewash, chrome-plated bronze combined with ½" polypropylene
Eyewash nozzlesLP11X: chrome-plated bronze, aerated water stream. LP11E: white and chrome ABS plastic
Nozzle operating pressure0.7 to 3 kg/cm²
Eyewash bowlMirror-polished stainless steel, ø 240 mm
ActivationStainless steel push plate
Valves½" ball valves and ½" pressure-regulating stop valve. Not self-closing
Inlet and outlet1" BSP thread
Flow rate1.5 L/min for 15 minutes, if the supply delivers the required pressure and flow
Mounting heightNozzles between 84 and 114 cm from the floor and at least 15 cm from the wall, per ANSI/ISEA Z358.1
OptionalStainless steel dust cover
