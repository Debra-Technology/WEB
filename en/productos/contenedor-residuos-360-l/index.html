360 L container · 2 wheels

360 L waste container

360-liter waste container made of virgin high-density polyethylene, with two solid rubber tread wheels and a galvanized steel axle. It is designed to be emptied by refuse collection trucks and withstands UV rays, cold, heat and chemicals. It can be customized with printing, heat transfer printing or screen printing, and is fully recyclable at the end of its service life.
